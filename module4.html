🏠 The 80% rule (coinsurance)

To get FULL replacement-cost payment on a partial loss, insure your home for at least 80% of its replacement cost. Insure less, and claims get scaled down proportionally (amount carried ÷ amount required) — an exam-favorite calculation.

✅ Replacement cost

Pays what it costs to rebuild/replace new — no depreciation subtracted.

📉 Actual cash value (ACV)

Replacement cost minus depreciation. Old roof = smaller check.
