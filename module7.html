⏳ RMDs: the new ages (SECURE 2.0)

🎂 Born 1951–1959

RMDs start at age 73.

🎂 Born 1960 or later

RMDs start at age 75 (beginning 2033).

⚠️ Miss an RMD?

Penalty is now 25% of the shortfall (down from 50%) — reduced to 10% if fixed quickly.

🧠
More SECURE 2.0 gold: Roth IRAs have NO lifetime RMDs — and since 2024, Roth 401(k)s don't either. First RMD can be delayed to April 1 of the year after reaching RMD age (but then you take two that year!).

Sources: IRS: RMD FAQs · Fidelity: SECURE 2.0 overview
