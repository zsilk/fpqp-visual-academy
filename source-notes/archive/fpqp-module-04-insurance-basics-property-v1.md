## Introduction (p. 136)
- The module discusses: various types of **personal risk**; the concept of **risk management** and the ways risk is addressed, including **risk transfer**; **principles of insurance**, insurance terminology, and contracts; the various **types of insurance companies**; the roles of **insurance agents and brokers**; and finally **property insurance** - specifically homeowners and automobile insurance.
