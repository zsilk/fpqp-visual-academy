## Introduction (p. 316)
- Many taxpayers turn to financial planners for tax planning advice. Beyond setting goals, planning investments, and preparing for retirement, a planner can help clients **minimize their taxes**.
- For most people, **income taxes are one of their largest annual expenses**. Lowering tax liability leaves more money to meet financial goals - typically by taking all available **deductions and tax credits**, using **tax-exempt investments**, and/or **deferring income** to the future.
- The module focuses on **how the federal income tax is calculated** - determining the types of income a client has and the tax treatment of that income, then considering available deductions, credits, and tax planning strategies.
- Note: some tax forms are included in the study materials as **visual aids only** - the exam does NOT test specific tax forms.
