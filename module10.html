👪 Meet the Riveras

Marco (38) & Elena (36) Rivera · two kids (6 and 3) · Marco earns $95,000 as an engineer; Elena earns $52,000 part-time.

💰 $8,000 in savings (≈ 1 month of expenses) 💳 $14,000 credit card debt at 24% 🏠 Mortgage — 28% of gross income 📈 Marco's 401(k): 4% contribution, 5% employer match available ❤️ No life insurance · no wills · 401(k) beneficiary: Marco's mother

🧭 How a planner thinks it through

1

🚨 Emergencies first

1 month of savings is thin — build toward 3–6 months (Module 2) while attacking the 24% credit card (guaranteed “return” by paying it off!).

2

🎁 Never leave free money

Marco contributes 4% but the match is 5% — raise to at least 5% for an instant 100% return on that slice (Module 7).

3

❤️ Protect the people

Two young kids + two incomes + no life insurance = big need. Answer: affordable term policies on both spouses (Module 5). Add disability coverage — the paycheck IS the plan.

4

📜 Paper the plan

Wills naming guardians for the kids, durable POAs, healthcare directives — and FIX that 401(k) beneficiary (Module 9: designations override the will!).

5

🔄 Monitor

Review yearly and at every life event — step 7 of the process never ends (Module 1).

🧠
The priority pyramid the exam rewards: ① emergency fund & toxic debt → ② full employer match → ③ insurance protection → ④ estate documents → ⑤ extra investing. Protection before growth!
