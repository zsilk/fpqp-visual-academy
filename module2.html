🧰 The emergency fund: your financial airbag

📐 The money ratios (memorize these!)

≤28%
Housing costs ÷ gross monthly income (PITI: principal, interest, taxes, insurance)
≤36%
Housing + ALL debt payments ÷ gross monthly income
≤20%
Consumer (non-housing) debt ÷ NET (take-home) income
🧠
Memory hook: “28 / 36 / 20” — house first (28 of gross), everything (36 of gross), consumer junk (20 of NET). Note the sneaky switch: the 20% ratio uses net income!

💳 What builds a credit score?

⚖️ Good debt vs. risky debt

🌱 Debt that can build wealth

Reasonable mortgage · student loans that raise income · business loans with a plan. Buys assets that can grow or earn.

🔥 Debt that burns wealth

Revolving credit card balances · payday loans · financing things that lose value fast. High rates, no asset left behind.

🆘 Bankruptcy: the two chapters to know

Chapter 7 — “fresh start” 🧹

Liquidation. Non-exempt assets are sold, most unsecured debts wiped out. Fast, but assets can be lost.

Chapter 13 — “wage earner’s plan” 📆

Reorganization. Keep assets, repay debts over 3–5 years from income under a court plan.

🧠
Memory hook: 7 letters in “LIQUIDS” → Chapter 7 = liquidation. “13” looks like a calendar page → Chapter 13 = payment plan over time.
