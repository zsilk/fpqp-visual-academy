⏳ Time value of money

ConceptFormulaUse it when…
Future valueFV = PV × (1 + i)ⁿGrowing one lump sum forward.
Present valuePV = FV ÷ (1 + i)ⁿDiscounting a future amount back to today.
Rule of 72years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rateFast mental estimate of doubling time.
Periodic ratei = annual rate ÷ periods; n = years × periodsAny non-annual compounding (÷12 for monthly).
Effective annual rateEAR = (1 + i/m)ᵐ − 1Comparing rates with different compounding.

📐 Cash-flow & debt ratios

RatioFormulaLimit
Housing (front-end)PITI ÷ gross monthly income≤ 28%
Total debt (back-end)all debt payments ÷ gross monthly income≤ 36%
Consumer debtnon-housing debt ÷ NET income≤ 20%
⚠️
Watch the switch: the 20% consumer-debt ratio uses net (take-home) income, while 28/36 use gross.

☂️ Insurance math

ConceptFormulaUse it when…
Coinsurance (80% rule)payout = (carried ÷ required) × lossHome insured below 80% of replacement cost.
Actual cash valueACV = replacement cost − depreciationOlder property claims without RC coverage.
Human life valuePV of future earningsSizing life insurance by lost income.

🧾 Tax & investing

ConceptFormulaUse it when…
Effective tax ratetotal tax ÷ total incomeAlways lower than the marginal (top) rate.
Taxable-equivalent yieldmuni rate ÷ (1 − tax bracket)Comparing muni bonds to taxable bonds.
Sharpe ratio(return − risk-free) ÷ std. deviationReturn earned per unit of total risk.
After-tax returnreturn × (1 − tax bracket)Comparing taxable investment outcomes.
