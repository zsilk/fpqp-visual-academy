# FPQP Curriculum Map

Scope and sequence for the Financial Paraplanner Qualified Professional (FPQP®)
program, administered by the College for Financial Planning (a Kaplan company).
These notes follow that topic order so study stays aligned with the exam's
domains.

> **On sourcing.** Everything in this notes set is written from scratch as
> original explanation of standard, publicly documented financial planning
> concepts — the CFP Board's seven-step process, IRS rules, insurance
> fundamentals, and so on. The official courseware is copyrighted and is not
> reproduced here. Use these notes alongside your own licensed copy of the
> course materials, not as a replacement for it.

## Modules

| # | Module | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 1 | The Financial Planning Process | Goals, the seven-step process, client psychology, ethics and regulation |
| 2 | Cash Management and the Use of Debt | Financial statements, credit, budgeting, business forms |
| 3 | Time Value of Money | Compounding, discounting, annuities, solving for unknowns |
| 4 | Risk Management and Property Insurance | Risk concepts, homeowners, auto, liability |
| 5 | Life, Health, and Disability Insurance | Policy types, needs analysis, annuities |
| 6 | Investment Basics and Strategies | Asset classes, risk/return, portfolio construction |
| 7 | Retirement Planning | Needs analysis, qualified plans, IRAs, Social Security, Medicare |
| 8 | Tax Implications of Financial Decisions | Income tax mechanics, deductions, credits, planning strategies |
| 9 | Estate Planning Basics | Wills, probate, transfer tools, trusts, transfer taxes, incapacity |
| 10 | Case Study | Integrative application of all prior modules |

All ten module titles are confirmed against the course table of contents. Module
3 is calculator-driven (HP 10bII+ and TI BAII+) and problem-heavy, so those notes
lean on worked examples more than the other modules.

## File Conventions

- One Markdown file per chapter, named `NN-slug.md`.
- Every file opens with YAML frontmatter carrying `id`, `module`, `chapter`,
  `title`, `tags`, and `see_also` — these drive the site's navigation and
  cross-linking without any parsing of prose.
- Section headings are stable: `## Overview`, `## Key Concepts`,
  `## Definitions`, `## Worked Examples`, `## Common Pitfalls`,
  `## Self-Check`, `## Connections`. A site generator can rely on them.
- Definitions live in tables so they can be lifted into a global glossary.
- Self-check answers sit inside `<details>` blocks: collapsed for humans,
  still present in the DOM for machines.
