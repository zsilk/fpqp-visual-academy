🎭 Annuity flavors: ordinary vs. due

📦 Ordinary annuity — pay at END

Payments land at the end of each period. Most loans work this way (your first mortgage payment comes after a month).

🏠 Annuity due — pay at START

Payments land at the beginning of each period. Think RENT — due on the 1st! Worth slightly more (each payment compounds longer).

🧠
Memory hook: “Rent is DUE on the 1st” → annuity due = beginning of period. On the calculator, that's BEGIN mode.
